Cinematic 16:9 hero illustration for a software product header.
Two-figure composition on a vast dark observatory backdrop.

COLOR PALETTE — STRICT (matches the product dashboard):
- Background: near-black #050505 to #0a0a0a. No pure black, no
  blue-purple sky, no neon.
- Primary accent: warm metallic gold #d4af37 (sphere, rings, lines).
- Secondary accent: deep amber #b8960f for gold shadows.
- Off-white #e8e8e8 for text/UI elements.
- DO NOT use bright red, neon blue, or any saturated cool color.

COMPOSITION (left to right):
- LEFT: a massive translucent golden orbital sphere, like a
  protective shell of energy. Concentric rings inside, faint
  inner glow, particle drift at the edges. Represents the 6-layer
  memory system.
- CENTER: a stoic Asian man in his 30s, short dark hair, goatee,
  black tactical jacket with multiple pockets and zippers over a
  black shirt, pendant on a chain, black fingerless glove on left
  hand, tactical pouch on belt, wristwatch or tactical device on
  right wrist. Holds a black folder with a small white geometric
  logo. Grounded contemplative pose, looking outward at the sphere.
  Calm, focused expression.
- RIGHT (behind him, slightly elevated): a semi-transparent ethereal
  female hologram, long flowing platinum-blonde hair, pale luminous
  skin, a small glowing mark beneath her left eye, form-fitting
  bodysuit with GLOWING GOLD (not blue) circuit patterns, star
  emblem on her chest, right arm extended outward gesturing.

HOLOGRAPHIC DISPLAYS (in gold only):
- A constellation-graph of gold dots connected by thin gold lines
  (the Kuzu knowledge graph / L5 layer).
- A timeline strip with gold dots and dotted lines threading through
  them (evolution chains: each new memory supersedes the previous).
- Small tabular readouts in fine MONOSPACED GLYPHS — but the
  text should be DECORATIVE, not legible words. They should look
  like the SHAPE of monospace text (small dashes, block-shapes,
  number-like forms) without actually spelling anything.

ATTRIBUTION (mandatory, must be visible):
- Lower-left corner, set in monospaced gold #d4af37 with thin gold
  rule above and below:

     HY-MEMORY · TENCENT

- Lower-right corner, matching monospaced gold with the same

     COMMUNITY IMPLEMENTATION

Both attribution lines should look like quiet, deliberate typeset

MOOD & LIGHTING:
- Volumetric gold god rays from above breaking through subtle haze.
- Quiet power, sophisticated, almost editorial — like a research-lab
  annual-report cover, not a movie poster.
- The figures' faces lit primarily by the gold sphere, with soft
  off-white fill ambient.
- A few embers or motes of gold dust drift in the air, very subtle.

STYLE:
- High-end concept art, photorealistic with a slight graphic-design
  finish on top.
- Skin, fabric, and the energy sphere all rendered with the same
  care.
- Sharp focus throughout, no motion blur, no lens distortion.

TECHNICAL:
- 16:9, ultra-detailed, 8K quality.
- The only text in the image is the two attribution lines spelled
