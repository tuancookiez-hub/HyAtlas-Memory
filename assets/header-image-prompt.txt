Cinematic 16:9 widescreen hero illustration for a software product
header. Two-figure composition on a vast dark observatory backdrop.

COLOR PALETTE — STRICT (matches the product dashboard exactly):
- Background: near-black with a faint warm gradient, around #050505
  fading to #0a0a0a at the edges. No pure black, no blue-purple sky.
- Primary accent: warm metallic gold #d4af37 — used for the
  protective energy sphere, the orbital rings, the connection lines,
  and the small accent details. This is the ONLY saturated color.
- Secondary accent: deep amber #b8960f for shadows on the gold.
- Text/UI elements: warm off-white #e8e8e8.
- Muted greys: #666666 for distant background details.
- DO NOT use bright red, neon blue, or any saturated cool color.
  The product's accent is gold, not red, not cyan.

COMPOSITION (left to right):
LEFT THIRD — A massive translucent golden orbital sphere, like a
protective shell of energy. Inside the sphere, glowing concentric
rings of gold light, reminiscent of a planetary system or an atom.
The sphere has subtle particle drift around its edges and a faint
inner glow. This represents the 6-layer memory system: each ring is
a layer, the shell is the cognitive architecture.

CENTER — A stoic Asian man in his 30s with short dark hair and a
goatee, wearing a black tactical jacket with multiple pockets and
zippers, layered over a black shirt. A pendant hangs from a chain
around his neck. He wears a black fingerless glove on his left
hand, a tactical pouch on his belt, and a wristwatch or tactical
device on his right wrist. He holds a black folder or tablet with a
small white geometric logo. He stands in a grounded, contemplative
pose, looking outward at the sphere. His expression is calm,
focused, the look of someone who has been at this a long time.

RIGHT THIRD — Behind and slightly elevated, a semi-transparent,
glowing ethereal female hologram with long flowing platinum-blonde
hair streaming dramatically, pale luminous skin, a small glowing
mark beneath her left eye. She wears a form-fitting bodysuit with
glowing circuit patterns in gold (NOT blue — gold). A star emblem
glows softly on her chest. Her right arm extends outward, hand
open, gesturing toward a complex holographic display.

HOLOGRAPHIC DISPLAYS:
- A constellation-graph of glowing gold dots connected by thin
  gold lines, representing the Kuzu knowledge graph (entities and
  their relationships, the L5 layer).
- A timeline strip showing small gold dots and dotted lines threading
  through them, representing evolution chains: each new memory
  supersedes the previous one, with the chain pointer visible.
- Small tabular readouts in fine JetBrains-Mono-style monospaced
  glyphs (NOT legible words, just the SHAPE of monospace text and
  numbers), representing the structured memory records.

ATTRIBUTION (mandatory, must be visible in the composition):
- In the lower-left corner, set in a refined sans-serif treatment
  (NOT a fake-UI block, NOT a screenshot-looking element, NOT a
  watermark), the text:

     HY-MEMORY · TENCENT

  set in monospaced gold #d4af37 letterspaced slightly, in a thin
  rule above and below, small and unobtrusive. This is the official
  framework attribution.

- In the lower-right corner, in matching small monospaced gold text:

     COMMUNITY IMPLEMENTATION

  with the same thin rule treatment. This is the project's own
  label.

The two attribution lines should look like quiet, deliberate typeset
credit, not like UI overlays. They should integrate with the
composition as design elements, not stickers.

MOOD & LIGHTING:
- Volumetric gold god rays from above, breaking through subtle haze
  in the air. The gold rays are the dominant light source.
- The whole image has a "quiet power" feel — sophisticated, almost
  editorial, not action-packed. Think product-hero, not movie-poster.
- A few embers or motes of gold dust drift in the air, very subtle.
- The figures' faces are lit primarily by the gold sphere, with
  soft fill from the off-white ambient.

STYLE:
- High-end concept art. Photorealistic rendering with a slight
  graphic-design finish on top — the image should feel like it
  could be on the cover of an annual report from a serious
  research lab, not a Marvel poster.
- Skin texture, fabric texture, and the energy sphere should all
  be rendered with the same level of care.
- Sharp focus throughout, no motion blur, no lens distortion.

TECHNICAL:
- 16:9 widescreen, ultra-detailed, 8K quality.
- No logos other than the one on the man's tablet (the small white
  geometric mark) and the two attribution lines described above.
- No fake UI screenshots, no buttons, no panels, no toolbars.
- No watermarks.

LONG VERSION INCLUDES: two figures, one human + one AI-hologram,
showing a human-AI partnership; a knowledge-graph constellation;
an evolution-chain timeline strip; a planetary/atomic energy sphere
representing the 6-layer cognitive architecture; gold god rays
